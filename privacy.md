# Privacy Policy for Sanqt

**Effective Date:** 29 October 2026  
**Last Updated:** 29 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

Sanqt (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a mobile application developed by Bhavik Makwana that lets you store loyalty cards, IDs, logins, documents, text notes, payment cards, lists, and similar items **entirely on your device**.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. Because Sanqt is designed to operate fully on the client side, we do **not** collect, store, process, or have access to the personal data you enter into the App.

This policy applies to the iOS and Android versions of the App available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

## 2. Our Core Privacy Commitment

- **All of your data stays on your device.** Loyalty cards, IDs, logins, documents, notes, payment card details (including expiry dates and CVVs), lists, and any other content you add remain in the App’s private, sandboxed storage on your phone or tablet.
- **Your data is encrypted by the App.** Everything you save is encrypted on your device with a key protected by your PIN, in addition to the encryption your phone provides.
- **We have no access to your data.** We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process the content you save in Sanqt, and the App does not send your content over the internet.
- **No accounts required.** You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or sign in with any third-party service to use the App.
- **No tracking or analytics.** We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or any other third-party SDKs that collect or transmit data about you or your use of the App.
- **Payments are handled by the stores.** When you purchase a Sanqt Plus subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We never see or store your payment card details.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect, receive, or store:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
- The content of any loyalty cards, IDs, logins, documents, notes, payment cards, or lists you save
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, or location data
- Usage statistics, analytics, or behavioral data
- Crash logs or diagnostic data
- Any data from other apps on your device

Information reaches us only if you choose to send it:

- **Email:** if you contact us for support, we receive what you write and your email address.
- **Feedback and brand requests:** the “Send feedback” and “Request a brand” options open a Google Form in your web browser. Anything you type into the form is sent to us through Google Forms and handled under [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App itself does not attach any of your saved content or device information to these forms.

## 4. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following types of information **locally only**, protected by the PIN or biometric authentication you set up:

- Loyalty cards, membership cards, and barcodes
- Identification documents and related scans
- Login credentials and generated passwords
- Payment cards (including expiry date and CVV when you choose to save them)
- Documents, notes, lists, and custom fields
- Expiry reminders and in-app notifications
- App settings and preferences (including auto-lock timing and the country used to suggest brands)
- Encrypted backup files that you create yourself

You can delete any or all of this data at any time from within the App, including erasing everything at once from Profile. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data (unless you have previously created and saved an encrypted backup file elsewhere).

**Your phone’s own backups:** on iPhone, the App’s encrypted data file may be included in your device’s iCloud or computer backup if you have those backups turned on. It stays encrypted with your PIN and cannot be read by us or by Apple. On Android, the App excludes its data from Google device backups.

**Encrypted backups:** The Plus feature that creates a password-protected backup file writes that file to a location you choose (for example, your device’s file system or a cloud storage app you select). The App does not upload or transmit the backup automatically. You control the password and the destination of the file. We cannot access or decrypt these backups.

## 5. Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions solely to provide its features. All processing happens on your device:

- **Camera** — to scan barcodes or capture images of cards and documents.
- **Biometrics / Face ID / Fingerprint** — to unlock the App (handled entirely by your device’s operating system; the App never receives or stores biometric data).
- **Notifications** — to remind you before a card, document, or ID expires. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Photos** — to pick an image you choose (for example, a screenshot of a card), or to save a card image to your photo library when you ask. The App does not browse your photo library.
- **Storage / Files** — only when you choose to import, export, share, or save a backup or document.

You can deny or revoke these permissions in your device settings. Core functionality that does not require a permission will continue to work.

## 6. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Sanqt Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.  

- Apple or Google processes the payment.
- The App checks whether your subscription is active with Apple or Google on your device. We do not receive your payment details, billing address, or Apple/Google account information.
- Apple and Google may provide us with standard, non-identifying sales reports (for example, the number of subscriptions in a country).
- No subscription data is linked to any personal information we hold, because we hold none.

## 7. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing SDKs.  

The only third parties involved are:

- **Apple** and **Google** — for app distribution and in-app purchase processing, under their own privacy policies. If you have chosen to share analytics or crash reports with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may pass those anonymised reports to us. You can turn this off in your device settings.
- **Google Forms** — only if you choose to send feedback or request a brand (see Section 3).
- Any cloud storage or file-sharing service **you** choose to use when saving or restoring a backup file (governed by that service’s privacy policy).

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Sanqt is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal data from any users, this risk is minimal. If you believe a child has provided information to us via support email, please contact us so we can delete it.

## 9. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, most data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, etc.) under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws do not apply to data stored inside the App — that data is under your exclusive control on your device.

If you contact us by email or through a feedback form, you may ask us to delete what you sent.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

## 10. International Users

The App is intended for global use. Because no personal data is transferred to us or to our servers, no international data transfers of your App content occur. Any support emails you send are received in India.

## 11. Security

Your data is protected by:

- Encryption by the App: your content is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key that is unlocked by your PIN (derived with PBKDF2-SHA256). Documents are encrypted individually.
- Device-level sandboxing and encryption provided by iOS and Android.
- The PIN or biometric lock you configure, with a temporary lock after repeated wrong PIN attempts.
- Password protection on any backup files you create.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device and PIN/biometric secure and for protecting any backup files you create. Because we never hold your PIN or your data, we cannot recover them if you forget your PIN.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. Material changes will be communicated via the App or the app-store listing where practical.
